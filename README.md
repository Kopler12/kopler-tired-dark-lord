"The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue".

## The entry

`main.py` writes one monologue of exactly 100 words to `/output/entry.txt`.
It is spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — the
tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections (the
stool instead of the throne, the queue behind the dragon, the grout in the
lava moat, the paperwork), never in a plain statement that he is weary.
He is still a villain: thirty years of ember pits, a tax on moonlight, a
goblin union negotiated with, and a duel offered with one condition — the
winner inherits the paperwork.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
